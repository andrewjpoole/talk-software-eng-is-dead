# Notes for hype-cycle

## gold rush

- 1850s, prospectors flocked to Sierra Nevada foothills to pan for gold
- none found any
- mining companies followed later, slower, more dangerous process
- 'Picks and shovels' outfits, selling tools and infra are biggest winners

## arms race

- escalating, competitive rivalry between two or more parties
- reactionary measures to obtain/maintain the top spot
- happening in model and tooling vendors
- also to some extent in enterprise more widely
- commercial FOMO/ fear of falling behind
